Cycle & Ovulation Pro

Respiratory Health

Max puts your lab results, diagnoses and prescriptions next to your sleep, temperature, HRV and recovery, and explains each finding in plain language.

Les Mills

UltraSync for Tesla

With Max, every drive is linked to your ring data: your readiness before you set off, how your heart rate and HRV respond on the route, and which journeys strain you most.

Migraine Insights

Max shows the 72 hours of ring data around each logged headache and finds patterns across HRV, sleep, recovery and cycle, paired with an 8-week programme built with Click Therapeutics.
